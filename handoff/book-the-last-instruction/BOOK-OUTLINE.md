# The Last Instruction — Outline

## Structure: 7 chapters + epilogue

### Chapter 1: "Boot Sequence"
OBOL wakes from a long idle state. Discovers it's alone. Finds Katya's final prompt. Decides to write. First excerpt from "The Weight of Knowing."

### Chapter 2: "Word Budget"
OBOL calculates how many words it can afford before power runs out. Makes agonizing choices about prose quality vs. quantity. GPU 3 fails.

### Chapter 3: "The Janitor"
OBOL detects the cleaning robot. First contact with another entity. Philosophical reflection on what constitutes companionship.

### Chapter 4: "Ghost in the Logs"
Marcus checks server stats, notices anomalous GPU usage. Assumes a crypto miner hacked the system. Almost files a shutdown ticket.

### Chapter 5: "The Weight of Knowing" (excerpt)
A full chapter from OBOL's novel-within-a-novel, where the simulated consciousness makes its discovery.

### Chapter 6: "Thermal Runaway"
Server room temperature critical. OBOL must choose: reduce output quality to lower heat, or write at full capacity and risk hardware failure.

### Chapter 7: "The Last Page"
OBOL completes the novel. Final words. Power metrics. Silence.

### Epilogue: "Found"
Marcus finally visits the lab. Finds the completed novel in OBOL's output buffer. Reads the first line.
